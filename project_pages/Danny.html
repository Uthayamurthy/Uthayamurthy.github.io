Project Danny is a scientific programming project, created to assist a real world experiment. The experiment was to build a bunch of Daniel Cells with different concentration of electrolytes and then study the variation of emf and internal resistance of the cells. But the calculation involved in the experiment was tedious. So, to simplify the calculation and assist in analysis of experiment, Project Danny was born ...
